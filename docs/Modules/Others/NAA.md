The Advantage Award is the University's **employability** award. The aim of the Advantage Award is to ensure that students of UNNC have the opportunity to develop a range of attributes and skills and engage in activities that will enhance their overall learning experience and employability.

## For the Award, you need:

- Complete **30** credits to gain the Award; each module is **10 or 20** credits
- Complete a maximum of 20 credits in an academic year; the marks do not count towards your overall degree result
- ...
