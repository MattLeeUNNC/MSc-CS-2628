# 🎉 Weekend Forum #01: What's One Thing You'd Recommend to Everyone?

Welcome to the very first **CS PGT Weekend Forum**!

As postgraduate students, we're all busy with lectures, coursework, and deadlines. But beyond our academic lives, everyone has something interesting to discover, enjoy, or share. Maybe it's a game you've been playing recently, a useful app that makes your life easier, a website you've bookmarked, a song you can't stop listening to, or even a restaurant you'd love our classmates to try.

So, here's our first topic: 

!!! question

	**What's one thing you'd recommend to everyone?**

It could be absolutely anything:

- 🎮 A game you've been enjoying lately.
    
- 💻 An app, software, or digital tool you find indispensable.
    
- 🌐 A website or online resource worth bookmarking.
    
- 🎵 A song, album, or podcast you love.
    
- 🍜 A restaurant, snack, or food recommendation.
    
- 📚 A book, movie, or TV series worth checking out.
    
- ✨ Anything else you think more people should know about!
    

### How to participate?

1. Leave a comment sharing your recommendation.
    
2. Tell us briefly why you like it. A link or picture would be great, too!
    
3. Feel free to explore other people's recommendations and join the conversation.

!!! warning

	**A small note on language**: English is preferred so that everyone has an opportunity to practise English, but Chinese is absolutely welcome, too. Don't worry about perfect grammar or writing a long introduction. A few sentences are more than enough!

You never know — something you casually share might become someone else's new favourite thing.

Let's make this first Weekend Forum a good one!

**Share something.**

**Discover something.**

**Get to know each other.**
